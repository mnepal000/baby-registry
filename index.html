How gifting works

  1. Pick a gift from the registry above, something you would love to give.
  2. Buy it through the store link on the card. Prices were checked when the list was made and may have changed slightly.
  3. Mark it as purchased with the button on the card, so other gifters know it is taken care of.

Purchased marks are saved in your own browser. If you grab one of the bigger gifts, do drop us a quick email too, so we can say thank you properly.

Questions

Where should gifts be shipped?

We live in the Washington DC metro area. For the shipping address, just send us a quick email and we will share it privately.

Should I include a gift receipt?

Yes, please. Gift receipts make exchanges painless if we end up with duplicates.

What if something is out of stock?

No worries at all. Anything similar from a trusted brand works, or pick another item from the list.

Can I give something that is not on the list?

Of course. Books, muslin cloths, and diapers in larger sizes are always welcome.

Can a few of us go in together on a bigger gift?

Absolutely, we love that idea. Just mark it as purchased once it is sorted so nobody duplicates it.
